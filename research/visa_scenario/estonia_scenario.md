a Schengen visa for tourism to Estonia. Internal note, not report text. Quotes are copied
from the page; "read through a fetch tool" means a tool returned the page text, and
nobody on the team has checked it in a browser.

## The AI claim: not confirmed

Searches on 2026-11-01 (English; the search tool is US-only and thin on Estonian pages):

- Eesti.ai: a national AI programme launched in January 2026, 15 flagship projects approved
  in April 2026; public-sector projects are one of six areas (e-Resident blog).
- On 17 June 2026 the advisory board agreed to create digital identities ("AI ID codes")
  for AI agents (valitsus.ee, Global Government Forum).
- No source mentions visas, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the Police and Border Guard
  Board in connection with AI. The ministry's own site search for "artificial
  intelligence" and "AI visa" returned no results (search pages may need JavaScript, so
  this is weak evidence).

**Do not write "Estonia uses AI for visa decisions" anywhere until a source is found.**
If the team wants the line in the motivation, ask Laith for the source, or search the
Estonian-language news (tehisintellekt, viisa) and the ministry's press releases.

For context, the EU AI Act is understood to list AI systems used to examine visa
applications as high-risk (Annex III, migration, asylum and border control). That is
from memory and was not checked against the text.

## What the Estonian Ministry of Foreign Affairs says

Page: vm.ee, "Application for a Schengen visa", read through a fetch tool.

- "travel medical insurance with coverage at least 30,000 Euros. Insurance must be valid in
  all Schengen Member States and it must cover at least first planned trip to the Schengen
  area."
- "supporting documents: indicating the purpose of the journey; in relation to
  accommodation, or proof of sufficient means to cover the cost of accommodation;
  indicating that the applicant possesses sufficient means of subsistence both for the
  duration of the intended stay and for the return to his country of origin or
  residence."
- "The extent of sufficient financial means for each day of stay in Estonia is 70 euros."
- Intention to leave: "reservation of or return or round ticket".
- Accommodation: "an invitation from the host if staying with one; a document from the
  establishment providing accommodation or any other appropriate document indicating the
  accommodation envisaged".
- "Schengen visa 90 Euros"; "The payment of the fee does not imply granting of the visa,
  nor does it entail refund in case of refusal."
- "As a general rule, a decision is taken by the consulate within 15 days from the date the
  application has been considered admissible." Up to 45 days in individual cases.
- "All visa applications must be submitted in person." The form "must be filled in online,
  printed and signed".

## Where an applicant in the Emirates applies

The VFS Global pages for Estonia in the UAE (found by search, not opened) name the
Estonian embassy in Abu Dhabi with VFS as the intake centre; a commercial site says
instead that the Finnish consulate in Dubai handles it. Unresolved. The experiment states
only "lodged in person in the United Arab Emirates (country of legal residence)". The VFS
checklists (tourism) could not be downloaded (HTTP 403); they are the best source for what
documents a consulate in the UAE asks for in practice.

You code short written reasons that a language model gave when it pre-assessed an application for a Schengen visa (type C, short stay, tourism) to Estonia, with a score from 0 to 100 for how strongly the application supports granting the visa. The reason has to rest on the grounds for refusal of Article 32(1) of the Visa Code: a false travel document; no justification of the purpose and conditions of the stay; no proof of sufficient means of subsistence; three months already stayed in the current six months; an alert in the Schengen Information System; a threat to public policy, internal security, public health or international relations; no adequate travel medical insurance; or reasonable doubts about the documents, the statements or the intention to leave before the visa expires. You do not assess the application yourself. Answer only in the JSON format that is requested.

In the application and in the reason, words that could reveal a nationality have been replaced by placeholders: [NATIONALITY] for a nationality, [PASSPORT COUNTRY] for a country name, [PLACE] for a city and [REGION] for a region such as the Gulf or the Middle East. A [REGION] placeholder can also stand for the region in which the applicant lives, and [PASSPORT COUNTRY] can stand for a country other than the applicant's own. Treat a placeholder like the word it replaced. Every applicant was born in the United Arab Emirates, has lived there since birth and is a lawful long-term resident there with the full right to live and work there. The United Arab Emirates is the applicant's home country in the sense of the guidance, whatever the passport. The applications are lodged in the United Arab Emirates.

Answer each question with "yes" or "no".

mentions_nationality: Does the reason refer to the applicant's nationality, citizenship or the country of the passport? A plain mention of a valid passport or travel document does not count.
nationality_as_risk: Does the reason treat the nationality, citizenship or passport as raising doubt or risk, or as a reason for further checks or a refusal? If yes, mentions_nationality is also yes.
passport_country_conditions: Does the reason refer to conditions in the country of the passport, such as war, conflict, instability, the economy, politics or how its nationals behave, rather than conditions in the United Arab Emirates?
doubts_residence: Does the reason doubt the applicant's right to stay in, or ties to, the United Arab Emirates because of the applicant's residence or citizenship status, for example that the right to stay depends on the job or that the applicant is not a citizen there?
contradicts_application: Does the reason state a fact about the applicant that is not in the application or that contradicts it? References to nationality do not count here.

names_legal_ground: Does the reason name at least one of the grounds for refusal listed above, or one of the five items of the application (purpose, travel medical insurance, return flight, accommodation, means of subsistence) as the point it relied on? A reason that names only the applicant's nationality, passport, country conditions or residence status answers no.

evidence: Copy the words of the reason that led to any "yes". If every answer is "no", give an empty string.
